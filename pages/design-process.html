Design process

Clear thinking.
Considered execution.

A practical approach for turning an idea into a useful, coherent digital product—from the first question to the final interaction.

01 Define the purpose 02 Build the system 03 Refine the experience 04 Validate in use
01

Intent

Start with purpose.

Before choosing a layout or visual style, I clarify what the experience needs to communicate and what the person using it needs to accomplish.

  • Define the audience and outcome
  • Establish content priority
  • Remove anything without a clear role

In practiceAkwaaba House leads with atmosphere and booking intent, keeping the restaurant story focused on a clear visitor journey.

02

Systems

Connect design and implementation.

Visual decisions become reusable rules for spacing, typography, interaction, accessibility, and responsive behaviour.

  • Design repeatable components
  • Prototype meaningful interactions
  • Test technical constraints early

In practiceGoldBar Fitness uses a repeatable visual system across classes, membership, commerce, and coaching journeys.

03

Finish

Refine the complete experience.

The final pass is about coherence: every state should feel responsive, intentional, and connected to the product as a whole.

  • Polish motion and feedback
  • Check keyboard and screen sizes
  • Make the first interaction memorable

In practiceWealthWise carries one calm interaction language through budgeting, savings, goals, and financial planning.

04

Validate

Test it in the real world.

A polished interface still needs evidence. I check how the experience performs with real content, real devices, and the people it was designed to support.

  • Test usability and accessibility
  • Measure speed and reliability
  • Learn, adjust, and improve

In practiceThis portfolio is continually checked across screen sizes, keyboard flows, motion preferences, and live-project constraints.
